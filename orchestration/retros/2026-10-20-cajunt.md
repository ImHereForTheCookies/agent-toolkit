## Three biggest sinks

1. **API-first tickets produced a product no user could drive (quality, ~2 rounds of eval and 6 fix tickets).** CAJ-6 (ROB-23) lists "UI" under Out of scope and sets `shelf_life_days` through a Python function only; CAJ-8 (ROB-25) lists "a plan UI" under Out of scope; no later ticket added a screen for shelf life, pantry edit/remove or the cook log. Every ticket passed review and QA on its own criteria (71% first-pass), yet round 1 found the core loop impossible in a browser and round 2 still found the brief's headline mechanic (perishables decay, planner uses them first) unreachable. The toolkit's own worked example (`examples/cajunt/`) models the same API-first split, and the decomposer had no step that maps each brief sentence to a UI path. Cost: 6 EVAL tickets (89 min of builds), a failed round 2 and a parked project.
2. **Round 1 reported gaps it did not ticket, and missed the headline one (quality).** The round-1 report lists "cook log view" under Spec coverage but ROB-35..40 do not include it (the brief caps fix tickets at 6 "merge small ones"); round 2 failed on it again. Round 1 also never exercised shelf life even though it is a sentence of the brief; round 2 found it. Cost: the second round could only report, not fix (rule: file nothing in round 2), so three known-small gaps (about 1.5 h of builds at the 10-20 pace) now wait for Robert's decision on ROB-40.
3. **Idle gates, not slow builds (time).** Tickets ROB-30..34 were created in Backlog with text that fails `lint_ticket.py`; the promotion gate correctly escalated them at 2026-10-19 00:36Z, Robert fixed them at 14:23Z, and the first one started 2026-10-20 10:13Z: 33.6 h with nothing building, against 157 min of actual work for the same 11 tickets. Earlier, the denied merges on 10-18 left 8 reviewed PRs stacked for about 12 h (05:34Z to 17:28Z) and needed extra re-stacking PRs (#9 to #13). Merges worked unattended from 10-20, so no change is proposed for the stack; the lint event happened outside the loop (hand-written tickets), so no loop change is proposed for it either (see "Watch" below).

## What went well (keep it)
- Per-ticket independent review plus QA caught real defects before merge: an API key leaking on redirect (ROB-25), non-finite quantities giving 500s (ROB-20), stock created through the API reading as 0 (ROB-23).
- The skeptical, user-script product eval did its job: round 1 found a core loop that no per-ticket check had exercised, with reproductions and screenshots; round 2 re-checked each finding and held the line on an unsatisfied core criterion instead of approving.
- The chained one-ticket-per-run pipeline built 11 tickets in 157 min on 10-20 once unblocked; stacking worked when merges were denied.
- Fixture-first tickets (ROB-21, ROB-32: fixtures committed before code) had zero fix rounds.
- Data survival (restart, reload) and zero-cost passed in both rounds.
